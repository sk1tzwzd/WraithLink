Duress & WraithVault

Three credentials, three jobs. Do not conflate them.

Credentials

You enterWhat happens
Your owner PINEveryday phone opens. WraithVault untouched.
Your panic wipe passwordGrapheneOS duress: encryption keys are destroyed and the whole device is wiped — including WraithVault. Use when you can burn the phone.
Your decoy PINOpens the everyday phone normally while silently destroying WraithVault (and other secondary users). For coercion while someone is watching. Requires a decoy-enabled build (ro.wraithlink.decoy=1).

The decoy PIN is not a replacement for panic wipe. Keep both when you use decoy builds. If a panic credential equals your owner credential, the owner one wins (GrapheneOS rule).

Decoy PIN status

Milestone 2. Published installer images stay lean (decoy off) until a dedicated decoy rebuild passes hardware QA. Decoy builds advertise ro.wraithlink.decoy=1; Setup then shows the decoy step and refuses to arm it until you confirm an off-phone vault backup.

Honest ceiling: useful against coercion and casual inspection of the unlocked UI. It does not undo a forensic image taken before unlock, and it is not “hidden” or deniable storage — WraithVault is a normal secondary user.

WraithVault

WraithVault is a secondary Android user profile created from Setup — not Android's "private space" (which can bypass VPN). Put sensitive apps there and give the profile its own strong lock.

It is not hidden from the lock-screen profile switcher, quick settings, or forensics. Treat it as a separate workspace you back up, not as deniable storage.

Back up before you destroy. On decoy builds, Setup requires tapping I've backed up before a decoy PIN can be set. Back up to X47 or an encrypted USB-C drive — never keep the only copy on the phone.

USB and backups

WraithLink blocks USB data while the screen is locked, which defeats forensic bridges. It does not block USB when unlocked, so your encrypted USB-C backup drive still mounts normally.

The honest limit

Not forensic-grade deniability. Nothing on the phone undoes a copy of storage taken before you unlock. For that threat, use the panic wipe. See the security model.
